## The exceptionalism lineage — resolved

The Villain pass found a 2024 "American-exceptionalism project" and could only
call it `STRONG_CONTEXT`. This session resolved it **by text match**.

`ESTABLISHED_ARCHIVAL_FINDING`: **it is not a separate project. It is Book 1.**

Probing Book 1's body for strings from the 2024 posts and outline:

| Probe | In Book 1 |
|---|---|
| 2024-09-11 post title *"The Indecent Exposure of American Exceptionalism"* | **yes — it is the Introduction's title** |
| 2024-09-11 post text *"shining city on a hill turns out to be"* | **yes** |
| 2024-08-22 post *"Ghosts of Genocide"* | **yes — Chapter 1** (retitled *Engineering American Innocence*) |
| 2024-09-12 *"Strings Attached"* | **yes — Chapter 7** |
| 2024-04-29 outline item *"Trail of Tears"* | **yes** |

So the classification the Villain pass could not make is now available:

> The 2024 American-exceptionalism material is **an early public serialisation
> of *American Imperialism Unhinged*.** Matt was drafting Book 1 and posting
> its chapters to Facebook as he went, August–September 2024.

**And the direction of the earlier inference was wrong.** The Villain pass
treated the Facebook posts as the project. Drive shows Books 1 and 2 were
created **2024-06-27**, seven weeks before the first chapter post. The posts
are output from an existing manuscript, not its origin.

**The ancestor above that** is *Manifesto for a Global Awakening*
(2024-04-29), whose outline contains "Justifying genocide of indigenous
peoples", "Belief in divine right to global dominance", and the Trail of Tears
material that reappears in Book 1.

### What this does and does not change

`SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION`. The Villain pass recorded the exceptionalism work
as `STRONG_CONTEXT` for the reform→indictment shift, explicitly not causal.
**That boundary still holds.** Knowing the 2024 project was Book 1 makes the
adjacency sharper — Matt was drafting an imperial-audit book in the exact weeks
he first put a deity in the dock — but it supplies no statement connecting
them. Q3 remains closed at `NO_CAUSAL_EVIDENCE`.
