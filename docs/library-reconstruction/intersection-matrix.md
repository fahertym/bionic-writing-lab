filling every cell would be padding. What follows is every edge where Matt has

material one side, gestures the other; **thin** = named only.

## The five most important

Chosen on mechanism, not topic size.

**1. education ↔ compliance ↔ consent.** The system produces the disposition it
later cites as agreement. This is the deepest edge in the corpus because it
runs in every book — Book 3 ch 3, Book 1 ch 6, Villain ch 12, and the 2011
coaching material — and because it is the mechanism that makes all the others
survivable for the people inside them.

**2. work ↔ deservingness ↔ disability.** Where the abstract argument becomes
material. It is also the edge where Matt has standing that no research can
supply, and the one currently buried in the trilogy's weakest book.

**3. empire ↔ innocence.** Not that empires do harm, but that they maintain an
industrial capacity for *not having done it* while the records remain public.

**4. legitimacy ↔ moral outsourcing.** The bridge from Villain to everything
else: once conscience is relocated to an unreviewable office, every other
mechanism gets cheaper to run.

**5. property ↔ enforcement ↔ borders.** Ownership as a continuous claim on
others' compliance. Strong on the property side, **thin on borders** — this is
the highest-value research gap in the corpus.
