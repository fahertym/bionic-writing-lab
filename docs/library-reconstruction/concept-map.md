**Session:** BWL Library Reconstruction, 2026-09-07

This extends the repository's existing `concepts/` ontology rather than
replacing it. Eleven concept nodes already exist in `concepts/*.json`. Six of

## The spine: five concepts everything else hangs from

`SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION`, built from artifacts dated across 2011–2026.

### 1. Legitimacy — how power gets permission

*Existing node: `legitimacy`.*

| When | Where | Form |
|---|---|---|
| 2011-03 | coaching message | a consequence is not legitimate merely because the coach imposed it |
| 2023-09 | *The Radical Carpenter* | Christianity's claim to Jesus is not self-authorising |
| 2024-06→2025-03 | trilogy | three introductions, all "unmasking" a claimed identity |
| 2024-09-06 | *"undeserving of worship"* | existence does not confer authority |
| 2025-26 | *Villain* | the label is doing the moral laundering |

**This is the oldest and most continuous concept in the corpus**, and the only
one with pre-2022 evidence. Its formulation is stable: *the thing that supplies
moral legitimacy is separable from the thing doing the acting.*

### 2. Institutional laundering — how harm is renamed

*Existing node: `institutional-laundering`.*

Book 1's whole apparatus: conquest → "collateral damage," extraction → "aid,"
domination → "leadership." Villain: atrocity → "judgment." Coaching 2011: the
same instruction phrased three ways producing different results in a body.

**The 2011 coaching instance is the earliest documented form and is not about
institutions at all.** It is about phrasing changing outcomes. The
institutional version is a later application.

### 3. Manufactured desert — who deserves what

Book 2's spine (the American Dream, meritocracy, "free" choice in coerced
work). Villain's inherited guilt. Book 3's provision chapters. The 2015
motivation essay about being "intimidated to try and fail."

*Existing nodes touching this: `survival-pressure`, `artificial-scarcity`.*

### 4. Internalized script — systems reproducing themselves through people

*No existing node. **This is the gap in the ontology.***

`ESTABLISHED_ARCHIVAL_FINDING`: this is **not** a new synthesis from the
Villain closeout, and it is **five months older than the previous session
said**. *Gospel of Liberation* Chapter 4 (October 2024) is built entirely on
it:

> "the seeds of the empire were planted deep within you. Its laws, its ways,
> its hierarchies — they live in your thoughts, your desires, and your fears...
> the empire is not only a system outside of you, but a poison that has seeped
> into your soul."

> "The greatest battle lies not in the streets, but in your hearts."

*The Narrative That Writes Us* (2025-02-21) then states it in essay form:

> "You were taught to see the world a certain way, to believe that things are
> the way they are because they must be... the systems we live under? They are
> stories. They persist because enough people believe in them."

Earlier appearances: Book 3 ch 3 *Education in Crisis — Manufacturing
Compliance* (2024); Book 1 ch 6 *Manufacturing Consent* (2024); Book 2 ch 1
*The Big Lie* (2024). **Gospel ch 4 (Oct 2024) is the earliest place it is the
whole subject rather than a mechanism inside another argument.**

**Provenance caution:** *The Narrative That Writes Us* still carries the
model's sign-off at the end of the file. Class B — the idea is his, the prose
is not evidence of his voice.

### 5. Preserved function — what survives after the audit

Steps 6–7 of the audit method. Villain Movement III. *The Great Unraveling*
(2024-09-27). ICN's entire existence. Book 2's conclusion *"Toward a New Vision
for America."* And — found this session — ***Gospel of Liberation* Chapter 5,
a five-article covenant that is the most explicit constructive statement in the
prose corpus.**

**Weaker in prose than in code, but no longer absent from prose.** See [`constructive-corpus.md`](constructive-corpus.md).

sides are listed. Resemblance alone is not a bridge.
