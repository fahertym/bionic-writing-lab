# Candidate Library

**Session:** BWL Library Reconstruction, 2026-09-07
**Not a publishing slate.** `MATT_DECISION` explicitly forbids converting this
into one. Ranked by evidence that a **genuinely distinct book-scale mechanism**
exists, not by topic appeal.

Ranking criterion: does the archive contain a mechanism that (a) recurs,
(b) cannot be stated as a chapter, and (c) has evidence on both sides of at
least one load-bearing intersection?

---

## Tier 1 — the mechanism is present and the material largely exists

**1. *The Villain in the Verse*** — done, editorial, not releaseable.
Mechanism: the label doing the moral laundering. 78,534 w.

**2. The Manufacture of Desert** *(≈ American Capitalism Unchecked, rebuilt)*
Mechanism: outcomes made to feel earned, so that redistribution feels like
theft and precarity feels like failure. **The most finished and most coherent
non-Villain manuscript** — ~47,500 w, ten chapters, a single argument. Needs ch
2 returned to a democracy book and evidence rebuilt to Villain's standard.

**3. The Manufacture of Innocence** *(new; assembled from Book 1 + Book 3)*
Mechanism: an industrial capacity for not-having-done-it, maintained while the
records stay public. Draws Book 1 chs 1, 6, 7 and Book 3 chs 3, 5.
**This is the strongest book in the corpus and it does not currently exist as a
book.** Its parts are distributed across two manuscripts, one of which stalled.

## Tier 2 — the mechanism is present, the material is thin or misfiled

**4. What Is Owed** *(new; Book 3's provision chapters, freed)*
Mechanism: a material floor as a condition of standing rather than as charity.
Book 3 chs 3, 4, 7, 8, 9 plus disability material scattered in posts.
**Currently buried inside the weakest book in the corpus.** The one book where
Matt has standing research cannot supply. `MATT_DECISION` needed on how much
personal material enters it — the Villain personal-writing protocol applies.

**5. Empire as a Lineage** *(≈ American Imperialism Unhinged, widened)*
Mechanism: the American case as one instalment of a Western institutional
lineage. Book 1's remainder, the 2025-07-09 Nazi-roots essay, the 2024-06-22
"comorbidities" post, 2023-11-26 Western-civ comments.
**Risk, stated plainly:** widening trades checkable American evidence for
civilisational generalisation. Only viable if the American record stays the
spine. See `american-trilogy-reassessment.md` §4.

**6. Gospel of Liberation** — ~93,500 w of 2024-10 sessions plus an 84 KB doc
**last modified 2025-12-30**, making it the most recently active pre-Villain
work. `UNKNOWN`: this session did not read it. Its size and recency mean it
could belong in Tier 1 or could be superseded by *Villain*. **Reading it is
the single highest-value unfinished research task.**

## Tier 3 — a real mechanism, insufficient material

**7. The Internalized Script** — systems reproducing through the people they
form. Ancestor: *The Narrative That Writes Us* (2025-02-21, class B).
Currently a seam running through every book rather than a book. `FUTURE_RESEARCH_HYPOTHESIS`:
this may be the *frame* for the library rather than a volume in it.

**8. Ownership of Cognition** — technology, compute, AI, who owns the means of
thinking. Book 2 ch 10 has outgrown its chapter; ICN supplies mechanism.
Thinnest prose, strongest implementation.

**9. Punishment** — eleven years of the best-evidenced reasoning in the corpus
(2011 coaching → Villain ch 12) and **no application to prisons.** The
mechanism is proven; the subject has never been written.
