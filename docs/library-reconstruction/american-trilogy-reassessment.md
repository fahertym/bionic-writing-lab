## 2. Does the three-book division still make sense?

**No — and Matt's own manuscripts say so.**

`ESTABLISHED_ARCHIVAL_FINDING`: Book 1 ends with *"Epilogue: The Hydra's Second
Head."* Book 2 ends with *"Epilogue: The Hydra's Third Head."* He was not
writing three books about three topics. He was writing **one animal with three
heads**, and he wrote that into the manuscripts.

Book 1 also carries an internal heading reading **"UNMASKING EMPIRE AND
CAPITALISM"** — the seam between Books 1 and 2 is visible inside Book 1.

The cross-contamination is systematic, not incidental:

| Book | Chapter | Belongs to |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 — *Slavery, Inc. — The Bloodstained Architecture of American Capitalism* | Book 2 |
| 1 | 8 — *Financial Vultures — Wall Street, IMF, World Bank* | Book 2 |
| 2 | 2 — *Plutocracy Rising — ... and the Death of Democracy* | Book 3 |
| 3 | 10 — *The Cult of Militarism — Endless War* | Book 1 |

Four of thirty chapters are filed under the wrong head. That is what happens
when the division is by **topic label** rather than by **mechanism**.

## 3. What was awkwardly crammed in

`SUPPORTED_INTERPRETATION`

**Book 3 is two books wearing one title.** Its Chapters 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9 —
education, atomised community, entertainment, infrastructure, elder care, food
insecurity — are not about democracy. They are about **what a society owes its
members and what happens when provision is withdrawn**. That is a distinct
book-scale argument, and it is the one place in the trilogy where Matt's own
material circumstances (disability, chronic pain, precarity) intersect the
political argument. It is currently buried inside a democracy book that stalled.

**Environment is a single chapter (2:5) carrying a planetary argument.**
*Profit Over Planet* is one chapter inside a capitalism book. Extraction as the
material substrate of empire is not a chapter-scale claim.

**Digital feudalism is a chapter (2:10) that has since outgrown its home.**
Written 2024. Since then Matt has built ICN and thought seriously about compute
ownership. Technology, ownership and AI now warrant separation.

**Manufacturing Consent (1:6) is the trilogy's real spine and is filed as a
case study.** How consent is produced is the mechanism underneath all three
books. It sits at chapter six of book one.

## 4. Is "American" the right analytical level?

**Matt already answered this, five days before he created Books 1 and 2.**

`ESTABLISHED_ARCHIVAL_FINDING` — Facebook post, 2024-06-22:

> "Because I don't just bitch about the US, it's all of Western civilization
> that is ill with the comorbidities of imperialism and capitalism. And it has
> metastasized as far-right populism and is spreading like a cancer across the
> globe."

Five days later: Books 1 and 2 created. He stated the correct frame, then
scoped down to a national one and wrote 124,000 words inside it.

Supporting evidence that the wider frame is where his thinking actually sits:

- 2023-11-26, twice: *"Anything promoting 'Western civilization' as superior,
  or more 'civilized' ... is white supremacist trash."*
- 2025-07-09, longform: *"Where Did the Nazis Get Their Ideology? The Western
  Roots of a Modern Atrocity"* — explicitly tracing an atrocity to Western
  rather than German lineage.

### Where widening strengthens the argument

**Empire.** The American case is one instalment of a longer institutional
lineage. Book 1's best chapters (genocide-and-innocence, manufactured consent,
foreign aid as leverage) are not American mechanisms; they are imperial ones
running on American hardware. Widening makes the pattern claim available
instead of merely implied.

**Race and classification.** Racial categorisation is not a US invention and
cannot be explained at the national level. Book 1 Chapter 1 needs the wider
frame to work.

**Property and enclosure.** The mechanism predates the United States by
centuries. Keeping it national forces the argument to start mid-story.

### Where widening weakens it

**Anything evidentiary.** The trilogy's force comes from specific,
checkable American records — budgets, court cases, corporate conduct, named
programmes. Villain proved that a case built on documents beats a case built
on patterns. A "Western civilization" book that trades named US evidence for
civilizational generalisation would be **weaker**, not stronger, and would risk
exactly the reverse-essentialism the brief warned about.

**Democracy and provision.** The specific failures in Book 3 — two-party
mechanics, a particular court system, this healthcare arrangement — are
genuinely national. Widening them dissolves them.

## 5. What the trilogy should become

`CURRENT_SYNTHESIS` — my recommendation, not a decision.

**Keep the American evidence. Change what the books are divided by.**

The old division is by *institution* (empire / economy / government). The
corpus wants a division by *mechanism*, because the mechanisms are what recur
and what the audit method actually operates on.

A structure the record supports:

1. **The Manufacture of Innocence** — how atrocity is reclassified as
   necessity, and consent produced. Draws on Book 1 chs 1, 6, 7 and Book 3 ch
   3, 5. *The strongest, most original book available, and it does not
   currently exist as a book.*
2. **The Manufacture of Desert** — how outcomes are made to feel earned.
   Essentially Book 2, tightened, with ch 2 returned to a democracy argument.
   *The most finished thing in the corpus.*
3. **What Is Owed** — provision, care, disability, precarity, the material
   floor. Book 3 chs 3, 4, 7, 8, 9, freed from a democracy frame they never
   fitted. *Currently buried in the weakest book.*
4. **Empire as a Lineage** — the widened frame, kept honest by American
   evidence. Book 1's remainder plus the 2025-07-09 Western-roots material.

Titles are placeholders. **The claim is the re-division, not the names.**

`MATT_DECISION` needed: whether the existing titles survive. They can. *American
Imperialism Unhinged*, *American Capitalism Unchecked* and *American Democracy
Undone* are serviceable titles for a differently organised argument, and the
brief already says the old structures do not have to survive.
