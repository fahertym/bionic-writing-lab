Matt Faherty

Bionic Writing Lab is my home for writing about the machinery underneath things: the systems that exhaust people, discipline bodies, launder violence into common sense, and make the built world feel inevitable.

It is not a brand funnel, a portfolio, a newsletter costume, or a feed pretending it has a memory. It is a place for work that needs more room than the scroll is willing to give it.

The Work

I write essays, fragments, pamphlets, fiction, and longer work about power, systems, bodies, memory, technology, coercion, dignity, and the structures people are taught to mistake for fate.

The pieces here are allowed to argue with each other, return to old pressure points, and stay connected over time. Some are finished. Some are rougher public artifacts. The point is not polish for its own sake. The point is keeping the shape of the work intact.

Bionic

Bionic does not mean artificial. It means assisted.

Archives, notes, drafts, transcripts, code, search, revision, and sometimes AI help unfold compressed thought into language that can be read, questioned, revised, and returned to. The tools are scaffolding. The writing is human.

Related Infrastructure Work

I also work on democratic infrastructure through the InterCooperative Network. That work is adjacent to this lab, not the whole point of it: ICN is institutional, governance, and economic coordination infrastructure; Bionic Writing Lab is where the writing lives.

The boundary matters. The lab can hold systems writing that makes adjacent infrastructure legible without turning every page into an advertisement for a project.
